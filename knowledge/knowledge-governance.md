# Atlas knowledge governance

## Permission-safe retrieval

Apply tenant and live page permissions before exposing a title, count, snippet, ranking signal, or AI context. Do not reveal that filtered candidates exist.

## Page history

Create immutable numbered versions for material changes. Restoring an older version produces a new current version and retains intervening history.

## Review ownership

Controlled content has an accountable owner and reviewer. Publishing is explicit, permission-checked, and audited.

## Grounded answers

The knowledge copilot cites readable pages and says when evidence is insufficient. It cannot edit content or change access.
